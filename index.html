REFERENCE · DECISION LOGIC

Why the planner gives these views

Each rule is drawn from your notes file. Rules vote on every opening; the weight shows how central the rule is. Where they disagree, the planner shows lower agreement instead of hiding it.

Important: This planner organizes chart observations into scenarios; it does not predict prices or guarantee outcomes. Square of 9 levels and pivot levels are reference points, not standalone entry signals. Market data may be delayed or unavailable. Verify all prices on your broker’s Nifty 50 chart and use a predefined stop-loss and position size.
